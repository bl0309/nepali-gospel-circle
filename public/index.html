About Our Ministry

We are a loving community of Nepali believers dedicated to growing in faith, fellowship, and service to our Lord Jesus Christ.

Our Heart

A place to worship, grow, and belong.

Nepali Gospel Circle is built around the presence of Jesus, the truth of Scripture, and the warmth of family. Come as you are and take your next step with us.

Biblical Teaching

Community Fellowship
